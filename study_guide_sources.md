### 3. Subject-Specific Tips & Strategies
- **GDC Efficiency (Casio fx-CG50)**:
  * **EQUA Mode (F4: Solver)**: Use to find solutions for complex trig equations within a specific domain without graphing.
  * **GRAPH Mode (F5: G-Solv)**: Find intersections, maxima, minima, and intercepts rapidly. Always check that the GDC is in **Radian Mode** (Shift + Menu -> Angle -> Rad) before the exam.
- **Algebraic Rigor in Section B**: Write down every intermediate step. Part marks are awarded for method even if the final GDC calculation is incorrect.
- **Induction Template**: Ensure the 4 steps are strictly followed:
  1. Base Case: Prove true for $n = 1$ (or starting integer).
  2. Inductive Hypothesis: Assume true for $n = k$.
  3. Inductive Step: Prove true for $n = k+1$ using the hypothesis.
  4. Conclusion: State the standard formal IB induction closing sentence.

### 4. Subject-Specific Tips & Strategies
- **The Twisting Strategy**: Guide the prompt towards three pre-memorized pillars:
  1. *Idealized Aspirations* (e.g., Gatsby's quest for Daisy, Amir's desire for Baba's validation).
  2. *Rigid Social Hierarchies* (e.g., East Egg vs. West Egg/Valley of Ashes, Pashtun-Hazara socio-cultural divides in Kabul).
  3. *Search for Moral Redemption* (e.g., Gatsby's tragic death/Nick's disillusioned elegy, Amir's restorative journey to save Sohrab).
- **The 25/25 Rule**: Avoid forcing keywords into sentences. Use elegant synonyms (e.g., *friction* for *conflict*, *temporal progression* for *time*) to maintain a natural academic flow.

### 4. Subject-Specific Tips & Strategies
- **The Register Rule**:
  * **Tú (Informal)**: Used for Diario, Carta Informal, Redes Sociales, Blog (directed at peers). Ensure all verbs end in `-as/-es` and pronouns are `te/tu`.
  * **Usted (Formal)**: Used for Carta Formal, Correo Formal, Artículo, Propuesta, Folleto. Ensure verbs end in `-a/-e / -an/-en` and pronouns are `se/su`.
  * **Warning**: Mixing registers will severely penalize Criterion C (Message/Structure).
- **Subjunctive Boosters**:
  1. *Es crucial que + [subjuntivo]* (Es crucial que tomemos medidas inmediatas).
  2. *Antes de que sea demasiado tarde* (Debemos actuar antes de que sea demasiado tarde).
  3. *Si tuviera la oportunidad, + [condicional]* (Si tuviera la oportunidad, participaría en el proyecto).
- **Paper 2 Reading & Listening**:
  * Skim headings and questions before listening or reading to identify context.
  * Listen for synonyms or paraphrased statements, as the correct option rarely uses the exact words from the audio.
