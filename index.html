🧠 Strategy & Mindset

  • The "Level Up" Protocol: Math and Physics are currently at 2s. We are fixing this entirely through Topical Past Papers. No passive reading. Do a question, check the mark scheme, understand the gap, repeat.
  • The 3-Step Study Debugging Protocol (For Stuck Questions):
    1. 3-Min Struggle: Look at formula booklet, sketch forces/graphs. Prime your brain before looking at solutions!
    2. Smart Peeking: Cover the mark scheme, peek *only* the first equation/line. Open notes/videos for 5-10m *only* on that specific sub-concept.
    3. Delayed Re-Test: Put a red star next to the question, erase it, and solve it again completely closed-book at the end of the day.
  • English P2 & Spanish Speaking Quote Strategy: Memorize 10 highly versatile quotes for Gatsby & Kite Runner (themes like power, class, isolation). In Spanish, learn 5 heavy-hitter phrases per Theme. *Always* write a thesis that directly answers the prompt, and start each paragraph with a clear link back to this thesis.
  • English P1 Speed-Writing (4 A4 Pages Blueprint): Don't panic! Budget 10m planning, 55m writing, 5m editing. Write a 5-paragraph analysis: 1 Intro, 3 Body (use PEEL: Point, Evidence, Explanation, Link), 1 Conclusion. You can easily fill 4 pages!
  • Pomodoro 50/10: During Study Leave, work for 50 minutes, then take a strict 10-minute break. Get up, walk, drink water.
  • Healthy Hours: Wake up at 8 AM. Stop studying by 8:30 PM. 11 PM - 12 AM bedtime. Sleep consolidates memory.
  • Guilt-Free Breaks: Your evenings are reserved for gaming and texting Mahi. When you rest, rest fully. When you work, work fully.
